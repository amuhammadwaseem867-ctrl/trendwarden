# trader-skill (third-party)

The `trader-*` folders in this directory are the Trader skill pack by
Jake Schincariol, copied unchanged from
https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/trader-skill (MIT licence, see
TRADER-SKILL-LICENSE).

They are Claude Code skills for **US stock investing** (SEC 13F copying,
Congress trade disclosures, earnings, news, budgeting, risk checks, tax lots,
rebalancing, and orders through Alpaca / Interactive Brokers with your
explicit approval). They are separate from the TrendWarden gold/crypto bot:
they don't change how the bot trades.

To update them, copy the folders again from the upstream repository.
